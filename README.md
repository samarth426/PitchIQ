# ⚽ PitchIQ

### Think Faster. Play Smarter. Understand the Game.

PitchIQ is an interactive soccer learning platform designed to help players develop their football IQ, improve tactical awareness, and make smarter decisions on the pitch.

Instead of simply memorizing soccer concepts, PitchIQ challenges players with realistic game situations, tactical scenarios, and interactive exercises that encourage them to think like footballers.

## 🎯 Project Vision

Great soccer players don't just rely on physical ability. They understand space, anticipate movements, recognize opportunities, and make decisions under pressure.

PitchIQ aims to make developing these skills more accessible through interactive, engaging, and practical soccer exercises.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Tactical Scenarios:** Analyze soccer situations and make decisions based on positioning, movement, and game awareness.
- **Visual Soccer Questions:** Learn through diagrams, player positioning, and illustrated game situations.
- **Keyboard-Based Challenges:** Use WASD or arrow keys to navigate interactive soccer scenarios and position yourself on the pitch.
- **Soccer Knowledge:** Test your understanding of soccer terminology, rules, tactics, and fundamentals.
- **Instant Feedback:** Understand your decisions and learn from mistakes.
- **Skill Development:** Practice recognizing spaces, understanding positioning, and making better in-game decisions.

## 🧠 What You'll Learn

PitchIQ focuses on developing important aspects of soccer intelligence:

- Spatial awareness
- Tactical positioning
- Decision-making
- Attacking movements
- Defensive awareness
- Passing and movement
- Soccer terminology and fundamentals
- Reading and understanding game situations

## 🎮 How It Works

1. Select an interactive challenge.
2. Analyze the presented soccer situation.
3. Use the available controls to make your decision or move into position.
4. Submit your answer.
5. Receive feedback and understand the tactical reasoning behind the correct decision.

The goal is not just to get questions right, but to understand *why* a particular decision works.
